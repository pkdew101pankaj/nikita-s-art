Meet The Artist

"Every hand is a blank canvas, and every bride carries a poetry of love."

I am Nikita (Instagram: @nikita.art25), the creative mind and lead artist behind Nikita's Art. For over 9 years, I have had the joyous honor of adorning brides and clients for their most cherished celebrations.

Unlike mass commercial henna services, my philosophy centers on purity and soulful storytelling. I personally prepare every batch of henna paste using premium triple-sifted Sojat henna leaves, pure eucalyptus, and cajeput essential oils - ensuring not only an exquisite dark mahogany stain, but also a rich, therapeutic aromatic experience.
